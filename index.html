Solera documentation

Solera is a Shopify theme for premium drinks: wine, spirits, specialty coffee and tea. This guide covers setup, every theme feature and the most common questions.

Getting started

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Find Solera in the Theme Store and select Try theme to add it to your theme library. You only pay when you publish it.
  3. Select Customize to open the theme editor.
  4. Choose a preset in Theme settings → Theme styles, then add your logo in Theme settings → Logo.
  5. Create your main menu and footer menu in Content → Menus. Solera uses the menus with the handles main-menu and footer by default.
  6. Preview your store on desktop and mobile, then select Publish.
Tip: before you publish a new version over a live store, duplicate your current theme so you can go back to it if needed.

Presets

Solera includes three presets. Each one can be fully customized after you install it.

  • Solera

    Dark and editorial, for wine and spirits. Age verification is on.

  • Tueste

    Warm espresso tones with rounded buttons, for coffee roasters.

  • Hoja

    A light palette with deep green, for tea, chocolate and pantry goods.

Open Theme settings (the brush icon in the editor) to change styles that apply across your store.

Home page sections

Add, remove and reorder sections from the left sidebar of the editor. Solera includes:

  • Vintage hero: a large headline with facts (origin, altitude, production) and an outline number over the image, such as a vintage year. Italic text in the heading is shown in the accent color.
  • Taste quiz: short questions that send shoppers to a filtered collection. See Taste quiz.
  • Featured collection, Collection list and Featured product.
  • Box builder: shoppers fill a case and see their savings. See Box builder.
  • Subscription plans: up to four plans, with an optional highlighted plan.
  • Story timeline: the steps of how your product is made.
  • Trust badges, Image with text, Rich text, Video, Blog posts, Newsletter and Custom Liquid.

Product page

The Product information section is built from blocks you can reorder: vendor, title, price, variant picker, buy buttons, pickup availability, inventory status, short notes, description, tasting scales, complementary products, collapsible rows, share and Custom Liquid. Apps can add their own blocks here too.

  • Price shows sale prices, unit prices and whether taxes are included.
  • Buy buttons include the quantity selector, dynamic checkout buttons, Shop Pay Installments and purchase options for subscriptions.
  • Pickup availability appears when you set up local pickup in Settings → Shipping and delivery.
  • Inventory status and low stock badges always use your real inventory. They only show when the variant tracks inventory.
  • Complementary products come from the Shopify Search & Discovery app.
  • Media supports images, videos, YouTube or Vimeo links and 3D models, with a button to view 3D models in your space on supported phones.

Below the main section, the default product template also includes Tasting profile, Variant comparison and Related products.

Tasting metafields

Solera reads product metafields in the custom namespace. Create them in Settings → Custom data → Products. Every field is optional: empty fields are hidden.

Selling coffee or tea? Rename the scales in the Tasting profile section (for example Body, Acidity, Sweetness, Bitterness). You can also connect each scale to any metafield you already use with the dynamic source button.

Vintages and comparison

If your product has an option such as Vintage, Solera shows it as large year buttons, shows the selected year over the first image and, for single-option products, the stock of each year.

  1. In the Product information section, set Vintage option name to the exact option name you use, such as Vintage, Year or Roast date.
  2. For the comparison table, create the variant metafields custom.compare_1, custom.compare_2 and custom.compare_3 (single line text), then rename the columns in the Variant comparison section.

Collections and filters

Collection and search pages support sorting and filtering by availability, price, product type, vendor, variant options and metafields. Choose which filters appear in the Search & Discovery app. Filtering updates the page without reloading, and every filter combination has its own address that shoppers can share.

Cart

  • Cart type: a drawer that slides in, or a full page.
  • Free shipping goal: enter the amount in your store currency. It must match your real shipping rates. The bar is hidden for shoppers browsing in another currency.
  • Gift wrap: create a product with one variant (for example "Gift box"), then select it in Theme settings → Cart. Shoppers add or remove it with one tap.
  • Order note, discounts, subscription details and accelerated checkout buttons are supported in the drawer and on the cart page.

Box builder

The Box builder section lets shoppers build a case from a collection of products without variants. The section shows three tiers, such as 3, 6 and 12 bottles.

Important: the theme shows the savings, but it does not create discounts. In Discounts, create automatic quantity discounts that match your tiers. Otherwise shoppers will see the full price at checkout.

Subscriptions

Install a subscription app (for example Shopify Subscriptions) and create your plans. Solera shows the purchase options on the product page, the plan name in the cart and the plan price everywhere. Use the Subscription plans section on the home page to present your club and link each plan to its product.

Age verification

Turn it on in Theme settings → Age verification. Shoppers confirm once and the answer is remembered on their device for the number of days you choose. It is hidden while you edit in the theme editor so it doesn't block your preview.

Note: this is a confirmation screen, not an identity check. Check the alcohol rules that apply in each country where you sell.

Taste quiz

  1. Add up to four questions in the Taste quiz section. Each answer has a label and a tag.
  2. Add those tags to your products, for example bold or red-meat.
  3. Choose the collection for the results. Shoppers land on that collection filtered by the tags they picked.

Languages and markets

Solera includes English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. To sell in more languages, add them in Settings → Languages. When you sell in more than one country or language, the selectors appear in the footer and the mobile menu.

Updating the theme

When a new version is released, Shopify shows an update notice in Online Store → Themes. Updating adds a new copy of the theme to your library with your settings and content. Review it before you publish. Code changes you made yourself are not carried over.

Custom code

Changes to theme code are not covered by support. If you edit the code:

  • Always duplicate your theme first and work on the copy.
  • Keep a list of your changes, because they are not carried over when you update the theme.
  • For larger changes, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner.

Support policy

  • We reply to every request within two business days, Monday to Friday.
  • Support covers theme setup, theme settings and bugs in the theme.
  • Support does not include custom code, design work, third-party apps or help with products that use the theme in ways it was not built for.
  • We fix confirmed bugs in a future update and tell you when it is available.

FAQ

Can I use Solera if I don't sell drinks?

Yes. The tasting scales, vintages and age verification are optional. Turn them off or remove their sections and Solera works as an editorial theme for any small or mid-size catalog.

Why isn't the tasting profile showing on my product?

The section only appears when at least one scale has a value from 1 to 5. Check that the metafield names match the table in Tasting metafields and that the values are whole numbers.

Why don't I see my vintages as large buttons?

The Vintage option name setting must match your option name exactly, without extra spaces. Uppercase and lowercase don't matter.

The box builder shows savings but checkout doesn't. What's wrong?

Create automatic discounts that match each tier in Discounts. See Box builder.

Why can't I see the free shipping bar?

Check that the goal in Theme settings → Cart is above 0 and that you are browsing in your store's main currency.

Where are the filters on my collection pages?

Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app and add filters there. Then check that Enable filtering and sorting is on in the Product grid section.

Does Solera work with review apps?

Yes. Add the review app block in the Product information section, or anywhere apps can add blocks.

Is the age verification legally enough?

It is a confirmation screen. Rules differ by country, so check the requirements where you sell and add any checks your checkout or shipping provider needs.

How do I change the fonts?

Go to Theme settings → Typography and choose any font from the Shopify font library for headings and body text.
